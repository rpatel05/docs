# Healthcare

## Connect patient engagement data with outcomes and operational systems

Healthcare organizations use Mixpanel to track patient engagement across portals, telehealth apps, and wellness platforms. The MCP server lets you combine behavioral analytics with scheduling, outcomes, and operational data — while maintaining the privacy-first approach your organization requires.

<Callout type="warning">
  **Compliance Note:** All data accessed through the Mixpanel MCP server respects your existing project permissions and data governance settings. For HIPAA-covered entities, ensure PHI is handled according to your BAA with Mixpanel and that any joined data sources maintain equivalent protections.
</Callout>

## Use Cases & Data Joins

### Portal Engagement × Appointment Adherence

> **The Question:** *"Do patients who actively use the patient portal have higher appointment completion rates?"*

| Data Sources | |
|---|---|
| Mixpanel | Portal usage events |
| Scheduling system | Appointment data |

**Business Value:** Quantify the link between digital engagement and care adherence to justify portal investment and guide outreach strategy.

### Telehealth UX × Patient Satisfaction

> **The Question:** *"Which parts of the telehealth flow have the highest drop-off, and how do those correlate with post-visit satisfaction scores?"*

| Data Sources | |
|---|---|
| Mixpanel | Telehealth funnel events |
| Survey platform | NPS/CSAT data |

**Business Value:** Pinpoint technical friction that directly impacts patient experience and prioritize fixes that improve both retention and satisfaction.

### Feature Adoption × Care Plan Compliance

> **The Question:** *"Are patients who use the medication reminder feature more likely to complete their care plan milestones?"*

| Data Sources | |
|---|---|
| Mixpanel | Feature usage |
| EHR/care management | Plan completion data |

**Business Value:** Demonstrate clinical value of product features to drive organizational buy-in for digital health investments.

### Onboarding Flow × Patient Demographics

> **The Question:** *"How does onboarding completion vary by age group and device type, and where are older patients dropping off?"*

| Data Sources | |
|---|---|
| Mixpanel | Onboarding funnel, device properties |
| Patient demographics | Age, location |

**Business Value:** Design inclusive digital experiences that ensure equitable access across all patient populations.

## Prompts by Role

### Product Manager

```text
Show me the telehealth visit completion funnel broken down by device type over the last 90 days
What's the 30-day retention for patients who completed onboarding in one session vs. multiple?
Which features have the highest weekly active usage among patients aged 65+?
Compare the appointment booking funnel for new patients vs. returning patients
What's the average time from account creation to first appointment booked?
Which onboarding step has the highest abandonment, and does it vary by insurance type?
Show me the adoption curve for our medication reminder feature since launch
What's the funnel from portal login → message sent → response viewed, by device type?
How does feature adoption differ between provider-referred vs. self-signup patients?
What percentage of patients who use secure messaging are retained at 90 days?
```

### Data Analyst

```text
Run a retention analysis for the Q4 2025 patient cohort, segmented by primary care type
What are the top 10 most frequent events and their week-over-week trends for 6 months?
Show me the frequency distribution for portal logins — how often do active patients log in per month?
Which user properties are most different between patients retained at Day 60 vs. churned?
Pull all events containing "telehealth" and show their daily trend for the past quarter
What data quality issues exist? Events with inconsistent property values or missing fields?
Compare engagement metrics across our three patient segments
What's the median session duration by platform, and has it changed since our last app update?
List all properties on "Appointment Booked" and show distribution of values
Run a segmentation of patients by age_group and show the top 5 events for each
```

### Clinical / Operations Lead

```text
What percentage of patients are completing full care plan milestones each month? Show the trend
Which departments have the lowest appointment completion rates based on portal engagement?
Are patients who engage with educational content more likely to complete follow-up visits?
Show me the weekly trend of prescription refill requests through the portal vs. phone calls
What's the no-show rate for telehealth vs. in-person, correlated with portal engagement?
Which patient populations are underutilizing the patient portal?
What's the average time between symptom checker usage and appointment booking?
Show me care plan compliance rates for patients using medication reminder vs. those not
How many patients actively use secure messaging, and what's the provider response time trend?
Which wellness program features have the highest engagement?
```

### Executive

```text
Patient engagement dashboard: monthly active patients, portal adoption, telehealth completion, 30-day retention — MoM and YoY
What's the overall digital health ROI story — engagement vs. care adherence metrics?
Which patient segments are growing fastest in digital adoption?
How does mobile app engagement compare to the web portal?
What are the leading indicators that a patient will disengage from our platform?
```
