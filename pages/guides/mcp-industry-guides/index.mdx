## MCP Maturity Model

Use this framework to understand where your team is today and what to unlock next.

| Level | Name | Description | Example |
|-------|------|-------------|---------|
| 1 | **Single Query** | Ask basic questions about one metric in Mixpanel. | *"What's our 7-day retention?"* |
| 2 | **Multi-Query Analysis** | Chain multiple Mixpanel queries to build a complete picture. | *"Show me retention by channel, then break down the top channel by feature usage."* |
| 3 | **Cross-Source Joins** | Connect Mixpanel with one additional data source for combined analysis. | *"Which users with high feature adoption also have open Salesforce opportunities?"* |
| 4 | **Automated Delivery** | Set up recurring MCP-powered reports delivered to Slack or other channels. | *Weekly metrics digest posted to your team's Slack every Monday.* |
| 5 | **Embedded in Decisions** | MCP analysis is part of standing rituals (sprint planning, QBRs, board reporting). | *Every product review starts with an MCP-generated impact summary.* |
